The final text should be written or reviewed by the client's lawyer. At minimum it
needs to cover:

1. **Contract & acceptance.** Ticking the box at signup is acceptance. The system
   records the version, time, IP and browser (`user_terms_acceptances`) and keeps
   them for 7 years.
2. **Roles.** Author, Publisher, Buyer, Superadmin and what each may do.
3. **Service fees.** 12.5% on direct sales; 11% / 10.5% / 10% on publisher sales by
   tier of distinct titles sold (§2.1). The fee is charged on the gross price.
4. **Royalties.** For publisher sales, the author royalty % is set by the
   publisher and accepted by the author. It is applied after the fee (§2.2).
5. **Payouts.** Paid on the 2nd and 4th Thursday. The earning window ends the
   Wednesday before at 23:59:59 EAT. Paid outside the platform by M-Pesa or bank.
   Lines can be held for a name mismatch, a dispute or suspected fraud (§2.5).
6. **Payout account name** must match the account holder's registered name (§2.6).
7. **Licence, not ownership.** Buyers get a personal, non-transferable licence
   to read on up to 3 authorised devices. No copying, sharing, screenshotting or
   redistribution. Copies carry a personal watermark.
8. **Anti-piracy monitoring.** Capture attempts on protected pages are logged
   with device details and may lead to suspension.
9. **Content rules & approval.** No book goes live without superadmin approval.
   The platform may delist books.
10. **Refunds, disputes, suspension, termination, liability, governing law (Kenya).**
